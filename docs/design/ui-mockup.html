What IR teams could get next, and the data each idea needs.

01

Index watch

Early warning when the share drifts toward an index exit. Gerresheimer fell 31% on 11 Feb and left the SDAX on 9 Apr.

have turnover, index lists · need free-float market cap, Selection List rank
02

Liquidity category monitor

Alert before the share falls out of Xetra category A and needs a Designated Sponsor (the Heidelberger Druck case).

have XLM, turnover · need nothing new
03

Announcement planner

Pick dates for results and capital markets days that avoid index reviews, expiries and peers’ results, when turnover is distorted.

have index review days · need peers’ financial calendars
04

Before-the-news monitor

Unusual turnover or options positioning in the days before an announcement, flagged for compliance.

have 1-min bars · need Eurex single-stock open interest (in the excerpt)
05

Buyback timing

When in the day the share is cheapest to trade, so a buyback moves the price least.

have 1-min bars, auctions · need nothing new
06

Surprise vs. consensus

Put the reaction next to how far results were from analyst expectations.

need consensus estimates (licensed)
